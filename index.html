Quick answers

Before you ask

Do I need to know anything about websites?

No. For the £1,400 build, I handle everything end to end and then train you on the simple bits — checking orders, changing a price. For the £350 pop-up, you'll need to be hands-on after handover — but you'll have my plain-English guides and a handover call, so you'll know exactly what you're doing.

What are the ongoing costs?

Shopify charges its own monthly subscription (their basic plan, paid directly to Shopify) plus standard card processing fees on each sale. That's it — I'll walk you through it so there are no surprises. My fee is one-off.

Can you really do it in 48 hours?

Yes — if you get me your products and photos quickly. The 48-hour clock starts when I have what I need from you, not when you first message.

What about selling football merchandise — is that allowed?

Generic fan gear (flags, party kits, unbranded merch) is fine. Anything carrying official logos, club crests or tournament trademarks needs a licence — I'll flag anything risky in your product list before we build, so you don't get your store or payments shut down.

What if I want the pop-up now and more later?

Smart play. If you upgrade to the full build within 60 days, your £350 comes off the price.

Why do you take a deposit?

It works for both of us. Your deposit books your slot and means I start your build immediately — and you only pay the balance after you've seen your finished store working. You're never paying everything upfront for a promise, and I'm never building for free. Fair both ways.

How do I know this isn't a scam?

Fair question — the internet's full of them.
